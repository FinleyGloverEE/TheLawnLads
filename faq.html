FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Rain, access, cuttings, paying, cancelling. If yours isn't here, just message me.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as I can get into the garden — side gate left unlocked, or tell me the code or where the key is — I'll crack on and message you when it's done.

What happens if it rains?

A bit of drizzle is fine. If it's properly wet, cutting does more harm than good — it tears the grass and churns up the lawn — so I'll message you and move you to the next dry day.

Do you take away the grass cuttings?

Yes, clippings are collected and taken away as standard. If you'd rather they went in your own green bin or compost heap, just say.

How long does a lawn cut take?

Most front or back gardens take somewhere between 30 minutes and an hour. Bigger or overgrown ones take longer — if you send photos, I'll tell you roughly how long when I quote.

Do you cut very long or overgrown grass?

Yes — have a look at the before and after above. Really long grass usually needs strimming down first and then a proper cut, so it's priced as a quote rather than a standard mow. Send a photo and I'll tell you what it needs.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. I bring my own mower, strimmer and bags for the clippings, so you don't need to lend me anything.

How often should my lawn be cut?

In spring and summer, every one to two weeks keeps it looking tidy — weekly if it grows fast or you like it really neat. It slows right down in autumn, and over winter it barely needs touching.

Do you offer one-off cuts?

Yep. Plenty of people just want it sorted once — before selling the house, before a party, or after being away. Book a cut online or send photos for a quote.

Do you offer regular lawn care?

Yes — weekly or fortnightly, ideally on the same day each time so you don't have to think about it. Mention it in your quote request and I'll sort out a regular slot.

What areas do you cover?

Hinckley, Burbage, Earl Shilton, Barwell, Stoke Golding and Sapcote. Use the postcode checker to check yours. If you're just outside, send a quote request anyway — if enough people nearby ask, I'll start covering it.

How do I pay?

Cash on the day or bank transfer, whichever's easier for you. I'll send the details with your confirmation.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Of course — just let me know as early as you can, ideally the day before. If you booked online, your confirmation email has a link to change or cancel it yourself.

What if I need extra work doing?

Just ask — hedges, weeds, a border that's got out of hand. I'll tell you the price before doing anything extra, so there are no surprises.

Can I send photos for a quote?

Please do — it's the quickest way to get a price. Use the quote form to add up to three photos, or send them on WhatsApp.
