## Privacy notice: The Lawn Lads

**Who we are.** The Lawn Lads is a lawn mowing and garden care business in Hinckley, Leicestershire, run by [owner's full name, or the responsible adult if the owner is under 18]. For anything about your personal information, email [quotes@thelawnlads.co.uk / another address] or call 07912 613180.

**What we collect and why.** When you ask for a quote on our website we collect your name, phone number, email address, postcode, the service you need, roughly how big your lawn is, anything you write about the job, and any garden photos you choose to add. We use them only to reply to you, give you a price and arrange the work. We don't use them for marketing, and we don't sell or share them with anyone for their own use.

Our legal basis is that you've asked us for a quote and we need these details to give you one and, if you go ahead, to do the job ([confirm: "steps at your request before entering into a contract", UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)]).

**Photos.** Your browser shrinks photos before sending them and removes hidden information such as the location they were taken. Please try not to include people in your photos.

**Where your information goes.**
- It's saved in The Lawn Lads' Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive) and emailed to our business inbox (Microsoft 365). Google and Microsoft store it for us.
- If you use the postcode checker, or type your postcode into the quote form, your browser looks the postcode up with **postcodes.io** to see whether we cover your area. Only the postcode is sent.
- To keep spam bots out, the quote form uses **Cloudflare Turnstile**, which checks information about your browser and connection (including your IP address) when you open the quote page. [Only include this line once the bot check is switched on in config.js.]
- If you choose to contact us on **WhatsApp** or book through **Cal.com**, those services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

The website doesn't use cookies, analytics or any tracking.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).
