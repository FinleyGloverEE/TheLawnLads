Privacy notice

What happens to the details you send when you ask for a quote: who sees them, how long they're kept and your rights.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Who we are

The Lawn Lads is a lawn mowing and garden care business in Hinckley, Leicestershire, run by Finley Glover, trading as The Lawn Lads. Finley is responsible for the personal information described on this page (the “data controller”).

For anything about your information, email quotes@thelawnlads.co.uk or call 07912 613180.

What we collect

When you send a quote request on this website we collect:

  • your name, phone number, email address and postcode
  • the service you need, roughly how big your lawn is, and anything you write about the job
  • any garden photos you choose to add
  • whether your postcode looks like it's in our area (worked out by your browser) and the time you sent the request

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email instead, we keep what you send us in the same way.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you, give you a price and, if you go ahead, arrange and do the work. The legal basis is that you've asked us to take these steps before entering into a contract, and then to carry out that contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

We don't use your details for marketing, we don't add you to mailing lists, and we don't sell or give your information to anyone for their own use. You don't have to give us your details, but we can't quote without a name, a way to contact you and your postcode.

Photos

Your browser shrinks photos before sending them and removes hidden information stored in them, such as the location where they were taken. Please try not to include people, car number plates or house numbers in your photos.

Who else handles your information

We use a few services to run the website and handle quotes. They handle information on our behalf, or as part of providing their service:

  • Google (Sheets, Drive, Gmail and Apps Script): quote requests and photos are saved in The Lawn Lads' Google account, and our quote form runs on Google's servers.
  • Microsoft 365: quote requests are emailed to our business inbox.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: checks that quote requests come from a person, not a spam bot. When you open the quote page it looks at your IP address and technical details about your browser and connection, only to tell people from bots.
  • postcodes.io: a free UK postcode lookup service. When you use the postcode checker, or type your postcode into the quote form, your browser sends the postcode to it (along with your IP address, as with any web request) to see whether we cover your area.
  • GitHub: hosts this website. Like most web hosts, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security.

If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, book through Cal.com, or follow our links to Facebook or TikTok, those services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare and GitHub may store or process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Each explains in its own privacy policy how it protects information transferred out of the UK.

How long we keep it

  • Quote requests that don't lead to any work are deleted 6 months after we receive them, including any photos and the copies of the emails. Our records are cleared out once a month.
  • If we do work for you, we keep your details and job records while you're a customer, and afterwards for as long as HMRC requires us to keep business records (currently at least 5 years after the 31 January tax return deadline for the year concerned).

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking. The only outside checks are the ones described above: the postcode lookup and, on the quote page, Cloudflare's bot check.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information (unless we have to keep it, for example for tax records)
  • stop or limit how we use it
  • give you your information in a format you can take elsewhere

It's free, and we'll reply within one month. Just get in touch using the details at the top of this page. If our bot check ever blocks you by mistake, you can always reach us on WhatsApp or by phone instead.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
